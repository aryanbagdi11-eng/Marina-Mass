About Us

Specialist matching,
not generic booking.

Most spa directories treat every massage the same. We built Marina Massage specifically because "just book me a massage" isn't the same as "I need someone trained in trigger point work."

Why we're specialist-first

A sports massage and a reflexology session require completely different training. Most booking platforms bundle every therapist under one generic "massage" listing, leaving guests to guess who's actually qualified for what they need. We match by specialty instead — sports, deep tissue, reflexology or trigger point — so the therapist you're matched with is actually trained for your specific request.

What we actually do

Marina Massage is an independent matching service — not a clinic ourselves. Tell us which specialty you need, and we connect you with a licensed, independently-run therapist in Dubai Marina who covers it. Sessions happen at the therapist's premises or, for mobile bookings, at your location.

How therapists join our network

Every therapist we work with holds a valid licence and is checked before we add them to a specific specialty category. We don't list a therapist under "trigger point therapy" unless that's genuinely part of their training.

Why it's free for guests

You never pay us. Therapists in our network cover a small referral fee only when a match actually leads to a booking — so our incentive is a correct specialty match, not a padded list.

Coverage area

We focus on Dubai Marina and the neighbourhoods within easy reach — Marina Walk, JBR, JLT and Bluewaters Island.
