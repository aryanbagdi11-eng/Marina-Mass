Common Questions

Before you
book.

Is Marina Massage a clinic?

No. We're a specialist-matching layer on top of licensed, independently-run therapists in Dubai Marina — sessions take place at their premises, or at yours for mobile bookings.

What is the difference between deep tissue and trigger point therapy?

Deep tissue works broadly across a muscle group with firm pressure. Trigger point therapy targets one specific knot at a time with more precise, localised pressure.

What does a reflexology session involve?

Reflexology focuses on pressure points in the feet believed to correspond to different areas of the body, aimed at full-body relaxation rather than direct muscle work.

Is this service free?

Yes, matching is completely free for guests. You pay the therapist's normal session rate directly.
